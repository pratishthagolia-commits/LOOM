Small steps, anywhere

Splits any page into 1–3 minute steps and highlights the key idea in each one.

02

Focus Mode

One step, one button, and a shrinking ring that makes time visible. Pause whenever life happens.

03

A buddy who checks in

Asks “doing okay?”, offers a new approach, and reminds you why you sat down.

04

Notes with deadlines

Park thoughts and due dates so your brain doesn't have to hold them. Reminders arrive before things are due.

05

Cozy sound

Lofi, rain and forest, right in the panel. Music lowers to half while it reads to you.

06

Read it to me

Not in the mood to read? Hear any step or summary out loud.

07

Kind breaks

After a stretch of focus: a calm moment, a breathing circle, no lectures.

08

Learns what works for you

Tap a face after each step. Too much? Steps shrink. Confusing? You get it in simpler words.
