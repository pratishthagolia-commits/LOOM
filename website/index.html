Get started

Install in one minute.

It runs as a normal Chrome extension, side by side with whatever you're studying.

Download for Chrome

Free · no account · your notes stay in your browser

  1. Unzip the download

    You'll get a folder called loom-extension.

  2. Open chrome://extensions

    Switch on Developer mode, top right.

  3. Click "Load unpacked"

    Choose the unzipped folder, then pin Loom from the puzzle-piece menu.

  4. Open a page, click the icon

    Press "Break this page into steps". Reload tabs that were already open so your buddy can join.

  5. See your progress

    Your stats and advanced settings live on the dashboard.

Why it works

Built with ADHD in mind.

Forgetting what to do and why you opened the tab. Drifting mid-paragraph. A wall of text that feels impossible to start. That's what we designed around.

  • One thing at a time. Big text, one primary button, low-stimulation colors.
  • Externalized memory. Your goal and notes are always in view.
  • No guilt, ever. No streak shaming. Your buddy celebrates and never scolds.
  • You're in control. Quiet mode, nudge frequency, text size, voice speed.
  • Private. Notes and settings stay in your browser. Page text is only sent for AI steps when you press the button.
